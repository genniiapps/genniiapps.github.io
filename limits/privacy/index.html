Gaugely Privacy policy Last updated: 18 November 2026. Gaugely was called Limits before version 1.1. Gaugely shows your own usage limits for Claude (claude.ai) and Codex (chatgpt.com) in a browser popup. What it reads Only when you allow access to a site, and only from that site: the usage percentages and reset times that the site's own usage page shows. For chatgpt.com it also asks the site for a short-lived sign-in token and sends it straight back to chatgpt.com in the single usage request. The token is kept in memory for that one request and is never stored or sent anywhere else. What it does not read Your chats, prompts, files, passwords, cookies or browsing history. What it keeps In your browser only (chrome.storage.local): the last percentages and reset times, the names of model limits the site reports (for example "Opus"), the time of the last check, a short history of percentages for the current 5-hour and weekly windows (at most 200 points each, used for the forecast), which notification thresholds have already fired in each window, your notification settings (four sets of thresholds, "when it resets", "forecast"), the chosen theme (also copied to the popup's own local storage so it opens in the right colours), the ID of your claude.ai organization (so it does not have to look it up every time), your settings, and small service notes about retries (when each site was last asked and how long to wait after an error). When you sign out of claude.ai, the organization ID is removed. When you turn a site off or take back its access, its organization ID and timing notes are removed; only a pause the site itself asked for (a rate limit or a human check) is kept until it ends. The answers of claude.ai and chatgpt.com also contain your name and email; Gaugely drops them right away and never stores them. Notifications Off until you turn them on. Chrome then asks for the optional notifications permission; turning notifications off gives it back. Notifications are shown by Chrome on your device; their text (service, limit, percent, reset time) and the small ring picture are made in your browser and are not sent anywhere. Where data goes Nowhere. Gaugely has no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not sell or share data. Requests it makes Every few minutes (1, 5 or 15, you choose), when you open the popup, press refresh, change a setting or grant access to a site, but a refresh you trigger never asks a site again within 30–60 seconds; one check of claude.ai is one or two requests, of chatgpt.com two: GET https://claude.ai/api/organizations, GET https://claude.ai/api/organizations//usage, GET https://chatgpt.com/api/auth/session, GET https://chatgpt.com/backend-api/wham/usage. Turning a site off in the settings stops all requests to it and gives the access back. Limited Use The use of information received from claude.ai and chatgpt.com complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
